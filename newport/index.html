The latest in Newport

November 12, 2026

Tonight, 6 to 7 PM, city staff and WSDOT hold the Newport neighborhood open house at Tibbetts Creek Manor, 750 17th Ave. NW. On the list: fish-passage progress, both phases of Newport Way from SR 900 to 54th Street, and the utility work that comes before the road closures. Event page

November 5, 2026

Tibbetts Valley Park, at 965 12th Ave NW, has five athletic fields, two of them lit; all are natural grass, open dawn to dusk. The city says it is launching a project in 2026 to re-envision the park, a framework for phased redevelopment; the planning stage is what is public now. Park fields · Re-envisioning project

October 29, 2026

City staff and WSDOT hold a Newport open house Thursday, November 12, 6 to 7 PM, at Tibbetts Creek Manor, 750 17th Ave. NW. On the table: fish-passage progress, Newport Way from SR 900 to 54th Street (Phases 1 and 2), utility work ahead of road closures, upcoming private construction, and one coordinated timeline for closures on Newport Way, NW Sammamish Road and West Lake Sammamish Parkway from 2027 to 2029. Event page

October 22, 2026

Two city advisory boards meet at Tibbetts Creek Manor, 750 17th Ave. NW, next week: the Park Board on Monday, October 26, at 7 PM, and the Transportation Advisory Board on Wednesday, October 28, at 6 PM. Both meetings are open in person or online, and both take verbal comments by email request and written comments. Park Board · Transportation Advisory Board

October 15, 2026

In Tibbetts Creek Valley, WSDOT has moved the SR 900 fish-passage work, Tibbetts Creek to Lake Sammamish, out of the I-90 project into a separate new project, likely to go out to bid in late 2026. WSDOT

October 12, 2026

Newport Way NW from SR 900 to NW Oakcrest Drive reaches final design for Phase 1 this month. The city replaces the water main and adds a sewer line in early 2027, ahead of road and trail construction starting fall 2027. Community meeting: Tuesday, November 17, 6 PM, Tibbetts Creek Manor. Project page Down the road in Tibbetts Creek Valley: the Environmental Board discusses a stormwater code update Wednesday, October 14, 6:30 PM, at Tibbetts Creek Manor, 750 17th Avenue NW. Next door, 30-acre Tibbetts Valley Park is the subject of a city project, started this year, to re-plan it. Agenda · Park re-visioning

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Issaquah newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Issaquah that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Issaquah's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It is shared with a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher, which may contact you about your home, and is never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
