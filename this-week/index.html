This week in Issaquah.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 7 of the Issaquah Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

What the city decided and what changed

The Highlands lights its big tree tonight

The third annual Holiday Tree Lighting runs 5 to 6:30 PM at Village Green Park, 2550 NE Park Drive, with free hot chocolate, cider and cookies, carolers, and photos with Santa and Mrs.

The budget was on Monday's council agenda; the outcome isn't posted yet

The November 16 agenda listed adoption of the 2027–28 budget and of the ordinance setting the 2027 property tax levy.

The Community Center closes all of next week

HVAC renovations close the Issaquah Community Center, 301 Rainier Blvd.

The Turkey Trot fills downtown next Thursday morning

The 5K starts at 9 AM Thursday, November 26, on a three-mile course through downtown Issaquah and benefits the Issaquah Food & Clothing Bank.

On the calendar

Sound Transit holiday schedules

Nov 26 to Jan 1 · Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day · Link light rail and ST Express. Link and ST Express run Sunday schedules on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day; on Nov 27 Link runs a Saturday schedule and ST Express a weekday one. Organizer's page

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

Nov 26 · All day · King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station. King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler. Organizer's page

Issaquah Reindeer Festival

Nov 27 to Dec 30 · Zoo hours; closed Dec 24-25 · Cougar Mountain Zoo, 19525 SE 54th St. The zoo's reindeer herd and Santa's house are the centerpiece of its December season. Organizer's page

Historically Hip Artisan Market and Shell Holiday Shop

Nov 28 to Dec 13 · 11 AM to 4 PM · Historic Shell Station, 232 Front St N. Local artists sell outside the Historic Shell Station on Small Business Saturday weekend and again Dec 12-13; the indoor Shell Holiday Shop is open Fridays to Sundays through New Year's. Organizer's page

Historically Hip Local Author Fair

Nov 29 · Noon to 3 PM · Downtown Issaquah. Local authors sell their own books for adults, teens and kids, hosted with the Issaquah Library and its Friends group. Organizer's page

Caroling at the Depot

Dec 3 · 6 to 7:30 PM · Issaquah Depot Museum, 78 1st Ave NE. A holiday sing-along at the old train depot, with Santa and Mrs. Claus for photos, hot chocolate and candy canes. Organizer's page

Lake Sammamish Holiday Market

Dec 5 · 10 AM to 2 PM · Meadow Shelter, Lake Sammamish State Park. The first holiday market in the park, with local makers, seasonal treats and native living trees to adopt. Organizer's page
