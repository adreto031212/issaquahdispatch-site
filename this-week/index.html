What the city decided and what changed

The council is expected to adopt the 2027–28 budget on Monday

The regular meeting is Monday, November 16, at 7 PM in Council Chambers, City Hall South; the city has said final adoption is anticipated that night.

NW Sammamish Road now stays closed until November 22, longer than the six weeks announced in October

The road then fully reopens for the holiday, from noon Wednesday, November 25, until Monday morning, November 30.

On the calendar

Issaquah Highlands Holiday Tree Lighting

Nov 19 · 5 to 6:30 PM; tree lit at 5:15 PM · Village Green Park, 2550 NE Park Dr. Mayor Mark Mullet lights the big tree, with carolers, Santa and Mrs. Claus, and police and fire crews on hand. Organizer's page

The Village Market Holiday Pop-Up

Nov 22 · 10 AM to 2 PM · Blakely Hall, 2550 NE Park Dr. Local makers sell jewelry, art, candles, ceramics, knitwear and baked goods. Organizer's page

Sound Transit holiday schedules

Nov 26 to Jan 1 · Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day · Link light rail and ST Express. Link and ST Express run Sunday schedules on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day; on Nov 27 Link runs a Saturday schedule and ST Express a weekday one. Organizer's page

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

Nov 26 · All day · King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station. King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler. Organizer's page

Issaquah Reindeer Festival

Nov 27 to Dec 30 · Zoo hours; closed Dec 24-25 · Cougar Mountain Zoo, 19525 SE 54th St. The zoo's reindeer herd and Santa's house are the centerpiece of its December season. Organizer's page
