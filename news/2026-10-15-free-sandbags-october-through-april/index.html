Issaquah · One useful thing

free sandbags, October through April

October 15, 2026 · Issaquah Dispatch, issue 2 · All Issaquah news

The city delivers sand and bags at no cost on request from October through April, 7:30 AM to 4 PM on working days, and during flood Phases II to IV: call 425-837-3470. Place bags 3 to 5 feet from your structures, never along property lines or stream banks; fill them two-thirds to three-quarters and interlock them. For Issaquah Creek warnings by text, email or phone, sign up for King County's Flood Alerts (information line 206-296-8200). City sandbags · King County Flood Alerts
