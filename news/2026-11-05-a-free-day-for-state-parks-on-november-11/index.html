Issaquah · One useful thing

a free day for state parks on November 11

November 5, 2026 · Issaquah Dispatch, issue 5 · All Issaquah news

Wednesday, November 11, is a state free day. No Discover Pass is needed to park at Lake Sammamish State Park or on other state-managed land. The free day does not cover overnight stays or rented facilities, so a camping spot still needs its pass or reservation. Plan the walk for the morning, when lots fill slowest. State free days
