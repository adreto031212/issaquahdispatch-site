What changed in Issaquah.

Every story the Issaquah Dispatch has run, newest first. Each links the public record it came from.

November 12, 2026

The council is expected to adopt the 2027–28 budget on Monday

The regular meeting is Monday, November 16, at 7 PM in Council Chambers, City Hall South; the city has said final adoption is anticipated that night.

NW Sammamish Road now stays closed until November 22, longer than the six weeks announced in October

The road then fully reopens for the holiday, from noon Wednesday, November 25, until Monday morning, November 30.

The Highlands lights its big tree next Thursday

The third annual Holiday Tree Lighting runs Thursday, November 19, 5 to 6:30 PM, at Village Green Park, 2550 NE Park Drive, with free hot chocolate, cider and cookies, carolers, and the mayor…

a free repair class and a tool library

Before you buy a tool for one job, borrow it.

November 5, 2026

The council expects to adopt the 2027–28 budget on November 16

The final budget hearing and the 2027 property tax levy hearing were set for Monday, November 2, at 7 PM in Council Chambers, 135 E. Sunset Way, and the city's calendar listing says adoption follows…

Update: Lake Tradition's accessible loop is due for new gravel in early November

City crews and the Washington Trails Association have fixed drainage and cleared brush on the Bus Trail and part of the Around the Lake Trail, off I-90 exit 20.

a free day for state parks on November 11

Wednesday, November 11, is a state free day.

October 29, 2026

The final budget hearing and the 2027 property tax levy hearing are Monday, November 2, at 7 PM

Any taxpayer can speak for or against the Mayor's proposed 2027–28 budget and next year's revenue sources, property tax included, in Council Chambers, 135 E. Sunset Way, or online by Webex.

Ballot drop boxes close Tuesday, November 3, at 8 PM

Issaquah's drop box is at City Hall, 130 E. Sunset Way.

Lake Tradition's accessible trail loop is getting new gravel, with work done in early November

City crews and the Washington Trails Association have fixed drainage, cleared brush and pulled out old viewing platforms on the Bus Trail and part of the Around the Lake Trail, off I-90 exit 20.

Issaquah is the first Washington city with a Charging Smart designation

The national program rated the city Silver for policies that support electric vehicles and widen access to charging.

City offices close Wednesday, November 11, for Veterans Day

VFW Post 3436 holds a service that morning, 11 to 11:45 AM, at the Issaquah Senior Center, 75 NE Creek Way, with a flag ceremony by the Liberty High School Junior ROTC. Service · Closure

test the smoke alarms when the clocks fall back

Clocks fall back an hour at 2 AM Sunday, November 1, an easy date to tie to the smoke alarms.

October 22, 2026

Your next chance to comment on the 2027–28 budget is Monday, October 26

Council takes public comment at its Committee of the Whole, 6:30 PM in Council Chambers, 135 E. Sunset Way.

Council kept the new 0.01% sales tax local

King County approved the tax to fund added services for children and families.

NW Sammamish Road stays closed until mid-November

WSDOT's utility work just west of Lake Sammamish State Park began October 5; the detour is West Lake Sammamish Parkway SE and I-90.

The new high school's name moves to a second survey

After the October 21 ranking night at Pickering Barn, the district's Survey 2 puts a shortlist of names and mascots online in late October or early November.

Arts grant applications close Tuesday, November 3, at 5 PM

Project grants are $2,500 or $5,000.

clear the storm drain before the November rain

Falling leaves block storm drains in fall and winter, and a blocked drain backs water up into streets and yards.

October 15, 2026

PSE's Grand Ridge substation in the Highlands gets its public hearing Wednesday, October 21

The Development Commission takes testimony at 6:30 PM in Council Chambers, 135 E. Sunset Way, or online by Webex, on the site development permit.

Council holds a hearing Monday on updated public records fees

At its 7 PM meeting on October 19, council takes testimony on a new fee schedule for records requests, including increases for police records and body-worn camera redaction.

The new high school's name gets ranked in person October 21

Suggestions closed October 14.

Detached ADUs now have a faster permit path

Issaquah is the first Washington city to let state-licensed architects self-certify that eligible detached accessory dwelling unit plans meet building code.

BOO at the Barn is Saturday, October 24

Pickering Barn runs two sessions: 2 to 4 PM with carnival games and crafts, and 6 to 8 PM with the Haunted Hay Barn, not recommended under age 6.

free sandbags, October through April

The city delivers sand and bags at no cost on request from October through April, 7:30 AM to 4 PM on working days, and during flood Phases II to IV: call 425-837-3470.

October 12, 2026

Council takes up a 0.01% sales tax tonight, and the rate you pay at the register stays the same

A special meeting at 6:30 PM Monday, October 12, in Council Chambers, 135 E. Sunset Way, considers an ordinance for children's and family services.

A $32 million water treatment plant goes to committee Tuesday, on the RV park's land

The Mobility & Infrastructure Committee meets at 6:30 PM on October 13 for an update on a central plant at the city-owned parcel at 650 1st Avenue NE, next to the Public Works campus.

NW Sammamish Road stays fully closed west of Lake Sammamish State Park until mid-November

WSDOT closed it October 5 for about six weeks to move utilities for a fish-passage project.

The 2027–28 city budget: two chances left to weigh in

Public comment at the Committee of the Whole on Monday, October 26, at 6:30 PM, and a final public hearing Monday, November 2, at 7 PM, the same night as the 2027 property tax levy hearing.

Green Issaquah Day, Saturday, October 17

Volunteers plant native trees and shrubs along the Rainier Trail at Confluence Park, 595 Rainier Boulevard N, and at Park Pointe, both 9 AM to noon; registration is required.

November 2, not October 31

The second half of 2026 King County property tax is due Monday, November 2.
