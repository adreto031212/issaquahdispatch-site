A $32 million water treatment plant goes to committee Tuesday, on the RV park's land

October 12, 2026 · Issaquah Dispatch, issue 1 · All Issaquah news

The Mobility & Infrastructure Committee meets at 6:30 PM on October 13 for an update on a central plant at the city-owned parcel at 650 1st Avenue NE, next to the Public Works campus. Building it means closing the Issaquah Village RV Park that leases the site. The plant would put the city's own wells back to work and cut purchased water from 50% of supply to 17%; startup is planned for 2034. Staff report
