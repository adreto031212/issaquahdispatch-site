Issaquah · Holiday guide 2026

Every Issaquah holiday date in one place.

Thu Nov 19 · 5 to 6:30 PM; tree lit at 5:15 PM

Issaquah Highlands Holiday Tree Lighting

Mayor Mark Mullet lights the big tree, with carolers, Santa and Mrs. Claus, and police and fire crews on hand.

Village Green Park, 2550 NE Park Dr · Free; free cocoa, cider and cookies · Organizer's page

Sun Nov 22 · 10 AM to 2 PM

The Village Market Holiday Pop-Up

Local makers sell jewelry, art, candles, ceramics, knitwear and baked goods.

Blakely Hall, 2550 NE Park Dr · Free to browse · Organizer's page

Thu Nov 26 – Fri Jan 1 · Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day

Sound Transit holiday schedules

Link and ST Express run Sunday schedules on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day; on Nov 27 Link runs a Saturday schedule and ST Express a weekday one.

Link light rail and ST Express · Regular fares · Organizer's page

Thu Nov 26 · All day

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler.

King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station · Organizer's page

Fri Nov 27 – Wed Dec 30 · Zoo hours; closed Dec 24-25

Issaquah Reindeer Festival

The zoo's reindeer herd and Santa's house are the centerpiece of its December season.

Cougar Mountain Zoo, 19525 SE 54th St · Zoo admission; tickets online or at the gate · Organizer's page

Sat Nov 28 – Sun Dec 13 · 11 AM to 4 PM

Historically Hip Artisan Market and Shell Holiday Shop

Local artists sell outside the Historic Shell Station on Small Business Saturday weekend and again Dec 12-13; the indoor Shell Holiday Shop is open Fridays to Sundays through New Year's.

Historic Shell Station, 232 Front St N · Free to browse · Organizer's page

Sun Nov 29 · Noon to 3 PM

Historically Hip Local Author Fair

Local authors sell their own books for adults, teens and kids, hosted with the Issaquah Library and its Friends group.

Downtown Issaquah · Free · Organizer's page

Thu Dec 3 · 6 to 7:30 PM

Caroling at the Depot

A holiday sing-along at the old train depot, with Santa and Mrs. Claus for photos, hot chocolate and candy canes.

Issaquah Depot Museum, 78 1st Ave NE · Free · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 5 · 10 AM to 2 PM

Lake Sammamish Holiday Market

The first holiday market in the park, with local makers, seasonal treats and native living trees to adopt.

Meadow Shelter, Lake Sammamish State Park · Free entry; Discover Pass required to park; living trees by donation ($100 for 2 ft, $200 for 4 ft) · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 12 · 10 AM to 3 PM

Infinity Farm Holiday Market

A free public holiday market at a family-run event farm.

Infinity Farm · Free · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 12 · 7 PM

Evergreen Philharmonic: Evening at the Pops

The Issaquah School District's honor orchestra plays music from Beauty and the Beast, Harry Potter, The Polar Express, Mamma Mia and La La Land.

Longman Performing Arts Center, Issaquah High School · Tickets online · Organizer's page

Mon Dec 21 · 1:30 to 7:30 PM

Yuletide on Front

A winter solstice afternoon of music indoors and out along Front Street; bring bells or an instrument to join in.

Front Street, historic downtown Issaquah · Free · Organizer's page
