Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Issaquah newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Issaquah that is news too.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, a Bellevue company owned by Adriano Tori. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It is shared with a local real estate brokerage owned by our publisher, which may contact you about your home, and is never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
