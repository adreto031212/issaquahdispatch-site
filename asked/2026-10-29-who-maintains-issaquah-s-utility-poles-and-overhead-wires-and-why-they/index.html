Who maintains Issaquah's utility poles and overhead wires, and why they aren't kept up better

October 29, 2026 · Issaquah Dispatch, issue 4 · Every answered question

A reader asked who maintains Issaquah's utility poles and overhead wires, and why they aren't kept up better. The electric utility, Puget Sound Energy, owns more than 350,000 poles across the Puget Sound area, and other companies run their own lines on those poles through PSE's joint-use program. PSE trims or removes trees that put its lines at risk, to meet state and federal requirements. If you see a tree close to a line, report it on PSE's tree-trimming form: a PSE-certified arborist checks it at no cost, and PSE usually answers within three business days. Urgent problems go to PSE Customer Service at 1-888-225-5773, emergencies to 911. One limit: PSE does not trim service lines, the wires from its pole to your house or meter pole.
